# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Order Processing and Handling Time

* **Processing Window:** All orders are subject to strict inventory verification, quality inspection, and packaging checks. Standard processing takes between **1 to 2 business days** (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).
* **High-Volume Periods:** During peak seasons, major promotional sales, or holidays, processing times may be extended by an additional 2-3 business days. Customers will be notified via email of any substantial delays.
* **Order Cut-Off Time:** Orders placed after 2:00 PM (Local Standard Time) will be processed starting the next business day. Orders are not processed, fulfilled, or shipped on weekends or official national holidays.

## 2. Shipping Rates, Methods, and Delivery Estimates

We partner with reliable, tier-one logistics providers to ensure secure delivery. Delivery timelines commence from the exact moment the package leaves our fulfillment center.

* **Standard Domestic Shipping:**
  * Estimated delivery time: 3 to 5 business days.
  * Flat rate: $5.00. 
  * **Free Shipping Qualification:** Orders subtotaling above $50.00 (after applicable discounts and before taxes) qualify for free standard shipping.
* **Express Domestic Shipping:**
  * Estimated delivery time: 1 to 2 business days.
  * Flat rate: $15.00.
  * Orders must be placed before the 12:00 PM cut-off time to qualify for same-day dispatch and next-day delivery guarantees.
* **International Shipping:**
  * Estimated delivery time: 7 to 21 business days, depending on the destination country and customs clearance procedures.
  * Rates are calculated dynamically at checkout based on package weight, volume, and destination address.

## 3. Order Tracking and Shipment Confirmation

* **Tracking Information:** As soon as your order is dispatched from our facility, you will receive an automated Shipment Confirmation email containing your tracking number and a direct link to the courier's tracking portal.
* **Tracking Responsibility:** It is the customer's responsibility to monitor the tracking status of their shipment. If there is a noticeable disruption or stall in transit, customers are expected to notify our support team or contact the carrier directly.

## 4. Delivery Protocols and Address Accuracy

* **Accurate Information:** Customers must provide complete, accurate, and valid shipping addresses (including apartment numbers, zip codes, and active phone numbers) during checkout. 
* **Incorrect Address Fees:** If a package is undeliverable or returned to sender due to an incorrect, incomplete, or invalid address provided by the customer, the customer will be held entirely responsible for all secondary shipping and handling fees required to resend the package.
* **Delivery Confirmation:** A shipment is officially deemed successfully delivered once the tracking system of the courier records a successful delivery confirmation scan at the specified destination address. 

## 5. Inspection, Lost, and Damaged Shipments

* **Mandatory Inspection Upon Arrival:** Upon receiving your package, you are required to inspect the outer packaging immediately for any signs of tampering, crushing, or water damage.
* **Lost Packages:** If tracking shows that your package has been delivered, but you have not received it, you must report the issue to us within **48 hours** of the stated delivery timestamp. We will open an official investigation with the courier. We are not liable for lost packages if the courier's delivery scan matches the address provided and no report is made within this window.
* **Transit Damage Claims:** If internal items arrive damaged or broken due to transit mishandling, you must retain all original packaging materials, boxes, and shipping labels, and notify us via email within **48 hours** with photo evidence to qualify for a free replacement or claim compensation.
