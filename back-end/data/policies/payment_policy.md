# Payment Policy

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods

To ensure a seamless checkout experience, we accept a variety of secure payment methods:
* **Credit and Debit Cards:** Major international and domestic cards, including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
* **Digital Wallets:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal for fast and encrypted express checkout.
* **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Available exclusively for select regional orders meeting specific order-value caps. COD customers must ensure exact physical cash is ready upon the courier's arrival.

## 2. Currency, Pricing, and Taxes

* **Base Currency:** All transactions processed on our store are denominated in United States Dollars (USD) unless explicitly stated otherwise via local store views.
* **Taxes and Duties:** Applicable sales taxes, value-added taxes (VAT), and import duties are calculated automatically at checkout based on the shipping destination and local government tax regulations.
* **Price Modifications:** We reserve the right to modify product prices, promotional discounts, and shipping fees at any time without prior notice. However, price changes will never affect orders that have already been confirmed and paid.

## 3. Payment Authorization and Security Protocols

* **Immediate Charge:** For credit cards, digital wallets, and online banking, funds are authorized and captured immediately upon placing the order. Production and fulfillment will only commence after successful payment authorization.
* **Data Encryption:** All sensitive financial data, credit card numbers, and billing credentials are fully encrypted using industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL) and Transport Layer Security (TLS) technology.
* **PCI-DSS Compliance:** We do not store, retain, or have direct access to raw credit card information on our servers. All card processing is securely offloaded to certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.

## 4. Fraud Prevention and Order Verification

* **Identity Verification:** To protect our customers and our business against fraudulent transactions, our automated security systems or compliance team may flag suspicious orders.
* **Additional Documentation:** If an order is flagged for manual review, we reserve the right to request additional verification documents from the cardholder (such as a government-issued photo ID or proof of billing address) before processing.
* **Order Cancellation for Fraud:** We reserve the sole right to cancel, hold, or refuse any order if unauthorized use, financial fraud, identity theft, or an illegal transaction is suspected.

## 5. Billing Accuracy and Disputes

* **Accurate Billing Information:** You agree to provide current, complete, and accurate purchase and billing information for all transactions. You must promptly update your account and payment details if changes occur.
* **Unauthorized Chargebacks:** Initiating a false, malicious, or unwarranted chargeback through your bank or credit card provider instead of resolving a legitimate issue through our customer support team will result in the immediate suspension of your account and potential legal recovery actions for incurred fees.
