# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and continually improve our services to you:

* **Personal Identification Information:** Full name, billing address, shipping address, email address, telephone number, and account login credentials.
* **Payment Information:** Credit or debit card numbers, billing names, and expiration dates. *Note: Full card details are processed directly by our certified payment gateway and are never stored on our local servers.*
* **Technical and Usage Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, time zone settings, and website browsing behavior collected via cookies and analytics tools.

## 2. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Data Collection

We use the collected personal information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:

* **Order Fulfillment:** Processing transactions, verifying payments, arranging shipping, and sending order confirmation and tracking updates.
* **Customer Support:** Communicating with you regarding inquiries, return requests, warranty claims, and providing technical assistance.
* **Account Management:** Maintaining your user profile, purchase history, and preference settings.
* **Marketing and Promotions:** Sending promotional newsletters, special discount offers, and product updates (only with your explicit opt-in consent; you may unsubscribe at any time).
* **Legal Compliance:** Complying with applicable tax laws, consumer protection regulations, and lawful government requests.

## 3. Data Security and Protection Measures

* **Technical Safeguards:** We employ robust administrative, physical, and technical security measures—including Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption, firewalls, and restricted database access—to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
* **Data Retention:** We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We value your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial gain. However, we may share data with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality agreements:

* **Service Providers:** Third-party logistics companies, payment gateways, cloud hosting providers, and email marketing services that assist us in operating our business.
* **Legal Authorities:** When required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights, property, or safety.

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

* **Right of Access & Rectification:** You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and request corrections to any inaccurate information.
* **Right to Erasure:** You can request the permanent deletion of your personal account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.
* **Right to Opt-Out:** You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of our emails.

## 6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
