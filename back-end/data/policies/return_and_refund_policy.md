# Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Eligibility Window and Timeframes
* **14-Day Return Window:** You may initiate a request for a return or exchange within **14 calendar days** from the exact delivery date confirmed by the courier tracking system.
* **Late Returns:** Any return requests submitted after the 14-day window will be automatically declined, and the items will be shipped back to the customer at their own expense.

## 2. Strict Item Condition Requirements
To qualify for a return, replacement, or refund, all items must strictly meet the following criteria:
* **Unused and Unworn:** The product must show zero signs of wear, usage, alteration, or washing.
* **Original Packaging:** Items must be returned in their exact, undamaged original product packaging, including all protective inner wrapping, branded boxes, and manuals.
* **Tags and Seals Intact:** All original hangtags, barcode labels, security seals, and warranty cards must remain completely attached and untampered with. Products with removed or broken security seals are strictly non-returnable.

## 3. Non-Returnable and Final Sale Items
The following categories of items are strictly excluded from our return and refund policy:
* **Perishable Goods:** Items that spoil quickly or have a short expiration date (e.g., food, flowers).
* **Intimate or Sanitary Goods:** Undergarments, swimwear, cosmetics, pierced jewelry, and personal care items once opened or unsealed.
* **Custom and Personalized Products:** Any item custom-made, engraved, printed, or specially ordered to your exact specifications.
* **Clearance and Final Sale Items:** Products marked explicitly as "Final Sale," "Clearance," or purchased during special warehouse liquidation events.
* **Digital Goods:** Downloadable software, digital gift cards, or online services.

## 4. Return Shipping Costs and Responsibilities
* **Standard Returns:** Unless the return is a result of our direct error (e.g., you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect item), the customer is entirely responsible for all return shipping costs, customs duties, and handling fees.
* **Shipping Method Requirement:** We strongly advise using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance. We cannot guarantee or assume liability that we will receive your returned item, and lost shipments will not qualify for a refund.
* **Original Shipping Fees:** Original shipping charges paid at the time of purchase are strictly non-refundable.

## 5. Inspection and Refund Processing
* **Mandatory Quality Check:** Upon receipt at our warehouse, all returned merchandise undergoes a rigorous 48-hour inspection by our Quality Assurance team to verify compliance with our conditions.
* **Approval Status:** If the returned item passes inspection, your refund will be formally approved. If the item fails inspection (e.g., shows signs of use, missing tags, or damage caused by the customer), the item will be rejected, and the customer must pay for re-shipping.
* **Method of Refund:** Approved refunds will be credited automatically back to the original method of payment (Credit Card, PayPal, etc.) within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on your financial institution's processing times.

## 6. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
* **Immediate Notification:** If you receive a product that is physically damaged, defective upon arrival, or incorrect, you must notify our Customer Support team via email within **48 hours** of delivery.
* **Evidence Required:** Clear photographic or video evidence showing the defect, damage, or wrong item alongside the original shipping label and packaging must be provided.
* **Full Resolution:** Upon verification, we will issue a prepaid return shipping label and provide a full refund or send a brand-new replacement at zero additional cost to you.
